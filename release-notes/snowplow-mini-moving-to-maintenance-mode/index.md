Following positive customer feedback since launching Snowplow _Micro_ as our recommended development environment, we’re directing all future development efforts to it.

Snowplow _Mini_ will enter Maintenance Mode on **August 11, 2026**. It will continue to run and remain accessible, but will receive only critical bug fixes, no new capabilities will be developed. Per our [lifecycle policy](/lifecycle-policy/), security patches may also lag behind actively maintained components during this period.

We plan to sunset Snowplow Mini completely at a future date, but not before giving at least **3 months’ additional notice**.

#### What this means for you

If you’re still using Snowplow Mini, now is the right time to plan your migration to the new development environment based on Snowplow Micro. If you haven’t already, you can review the [announcement and migration guidance here](/release-notes/meet-the-new-development-environments/).

If you have any questions or concerns please reach out to your Snowplow Account team or [suppport@snowplow.io](mailto:suppport@snowplow.io)
