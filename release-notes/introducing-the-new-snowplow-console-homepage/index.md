We're excited to announce the release of a brand-new homepage for Console – your central hub for understanding the state of your Snowplow setup.

With this release, you’ll be able to:

* Get a clear overview of your pipeline’s health and status.
* Monitor event and failed event volumes at a glance.
* Get instant visibility into common data quality issues.
* See counts and overviews across your data products, data structures, and data models

Explore the new experience!
