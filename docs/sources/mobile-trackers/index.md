Use our Snowplow [Android](https://github.com/snowplow/snowplow-android-tracker) and [iOS](https://github.com/snowplow/snowplow-ios-tracker) trackers to collect data from Android, iOS, macOS, tvOS and watchOS apps.

:::tip[Add the tracker with your AI assistant]
If you use Snowplow CDI, a coding agent connected to the Snowplow MCP server can add the Android or iOS tracker to your app and check that the events arrive in your pipeline. For example: "Add the Snowplow iOS tracker to this app, track screen views, and confirm the events reach my development pipeline."
