The Signals free tier gives you a Snowplow-hosted environment for building real-time customer attributes and interventions with [Signals](/docs/signals/introduction/index.md). It uses the same environment as the [Snowplow free trial](/docs/get-started/free-trial/index.md), but has no end date. Instead, usage caps apply.

Use the free tier to build and run Signals use cases on an ongoing basis without setting up any cloud infrastructure yourself.

## Sign up for the free tier

Sign up for the Signals free tier on the [Signals website](https://signals.snowplow.io/start?utm_source=docs.snowplow.io&utm_medium=referral). It needs no credit card.

After you sign up, your environment is provisioned automatically. You will receive access instructions within around 20 minutes.

## Usage caps

The Signals free tier doesn't expire, but these caps apply:

* A maximum of 5 million events
* A maximum of 1 GB of attribute memory

## Other limitations

The free tier has the same product and capacity limitations as the [Snowplow free trial](/docs/get-started/free-trial/index.md):

* First-party tracking using your own domain is not available
* Some destinations are not available. [Contact sales](https://console.snowplowanalytics.com/contact-us) to discuss your requirements.
* Collector-level rate limits apply, capped at around 20 events per second
* Pipeline, Signals, and [Identities](/docs/identities/index.md) resources are limited and do not scale
