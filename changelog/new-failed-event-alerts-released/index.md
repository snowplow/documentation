We are excited to introduce **Failed Event Alerts**, a new enhancement to the **Data Quality Dashboard**. This feature allows you to create targeted, workflow-integrated alerts for failed events, enabling your teams to proactively manage and resolve data quality issues faster.

By sending context-rich notifications directly to your team's communication channels, you can turn data quality monitoring into a seamless and actionable part of your daily operations. This ensures that your data pipelines remain healthy and your downstream use cases are powered by reliable, high-quality data.

---

## Key Capabilities

* **Filterable Alerts**: Create highly specific alerts by filtering on **App ID**, **Issue Type**, or **Data Structure**. This routes notifications directly to the teams responsible for those data streams, reducing noise and strengthening data ownership.

* **Workflow Integrations**: Deliver alerts directly into the tools your teams use every day. Current integrations include:

  * **Email**: Send detailed notifications to one or more recipients.

  * **Slack**: Pipe alerts to a dedicated Slack channel for immediate team-wide visibility.

* **Context-Rich Notifications**: Every alert contains the details needed to begin an investigation, accelerating time-to-resolution.

* **\[Coming Soon] Volume-Based Thresholds**: In an upcoming release, triggering alerts based on thresholds within a set time interval will be enabled, (ie: over 1,000 events within 10 minutes), helping teams further instantly distinguish between minor issues and critical problems.
